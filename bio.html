BIO

Moceol speaks English and Efik fluently, and has loved music all his life. He began experimenting with different sounds in his school days until he arrived at his own unique sound, a blend of rap, hip hop, R&B and more.

With his eyes set on the top of the entertainment industry, Moceol got into sound engineering in 2018 and has steadily worked not only on his music but also on his stage performance.

In 2021, he showed the world his musical proficiency with his first official single, “Butterfly Effect” featuring Ira, and he has been working ever since to put his name out there globally.
